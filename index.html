Profile 个人简介

Systems research with production pressure.面向真实云基础设施压力的系统研究。

I am a Lecturer in the School of Computer Science and Technology at Huazhong University of Science and Technology (HUST). I received my B.S. degree in Software Engineering in 2016 and my Ph.D. degree in Computer Science and Technology in 2022 from HUST, advised by Prof. Song Wu. From 2018 to 2019, I was supported by the China Scholarship Council for joint doctoral training at the University of Texas at Arlington, working with Prof. Jia Rao.

Before returning to HUST, I worked in Alibaba Cloud Infrastructure from 2022 to 2025, where I led virtualization architecture upgrades for secure container systems and supported large-scale production container workloads. My current research focuses on operating systems, virtualization, cloud-native systems, and AI infrastructure.

我现任华中科技大学计算机科学与技术学院讲师。2016 年于华中科技大学获软件工程学士学位，2022 年获计算机科学与技术博士学位，导师为吴松教授。2018-2019 年获国家留学基金委资助，赴德克萨斯大学阿灵顿分校联合培养，合作导师为饶嘉教授。

2022-2025 年曾任职于阿里云基础设施事业部，主导完成阿里云安全容器系统的虚拟化架构升级，并支撑日均百万核规模的容器实例稳定运行。目前研究聚焦操作系统、虚拟化、云原生系统与 AI 基础设施。

Research 研究方向

From kernel abstractions to AI-native cloud systems.从内核抽象到 AI 原生云系统。

KERNEL

Operating Systems操作系统机制

Kernel support for scheduling, memory management, fault tolerance, and predictable performance in multi-tenant cloud environments.研究多租户云环境中的调度、内存管理、故障容忍与可预测性能等内核机制。

VIRT

Virtualization and Secure Containers虚拟化与安全容器

Efficient nested virtualization, shadow paging, and isolation techniques for secure containers deployed at cloud scale.面向大规模云部署的高效嵌套虚拟化、影子页表与安全容器隔离技术。

CLOUD

Serverless and Cloud-native SystemsServerless 与云原生系统

Runtime and resource management for elastic workloads, containers, distributed shared memory, and cloud-native services.面向弹性负载、容器、分布式共享内存与云原生服务的运行时与资源管理。

AI

Cloud Systems for AI面向 AI 的云计算系统

System software for LLM inference, agent execution, sandboxing, and large-scale AI infrastructure.研究大模型推理、智能体执行、沙箱隔离与大规模 AI 基础设施中的系统软件问题。
